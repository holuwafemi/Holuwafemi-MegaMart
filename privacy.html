Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Holuwafemi Mega Store ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our customers and vendors, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what rights you have.

1. What We Collect

DataCollected WhenWhy
Name, phone number, emailSignup, checkoutAccount creation, order communication
Delivery addressCheckoutFulfilling your order
Order & payment historyPlacing an orderOrder tracking, receipts, refunds
Business name, product photosVendors applying to sellSeller verification & storefront listing
We do not collect or store your card number — payments are processed directly by Paystack, a licensed payment processor. We only receive a payment reference and status.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To communicate with you about orders, via WhatsApp, SMS, or email
  • To verify and manage vendor accounts
  • To improve our store and prevent fraud

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored securely with Google Firebase, encrypted both in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by security rules so that customers and vendors can only see their own information, and only our team can access the full store record.

4. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Active account data: kept while your account is active
  • Order records: kept for as long as required for tax, warranty, and dispute purposes
  • If you delete your account, we remove your personal profile data; order records may be retained in anonymized/aggregate form where required by law

5. Your Rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal/record-keeping obligations
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

6. Vendors

If you apply to sell on Holuwafemi Mega Store, we collect your business details and contact information to verify your account. Approved vendors can only access and manage their own product listings and orders — never other vendors' data.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy questions, data requests, or to exercise your rights above, contact us via WhatsApp through the "Sell on Holuwafemi" / Help links on our site, or at [insert your support email/phone here].
